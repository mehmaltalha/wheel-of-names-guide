# Spin the Wheel Online: Free Random Name Picker for Classrooms and Giveaways

Everyone has faced it: who goes first, who wins the prize, who gets picked. A [random name picker](https://wheelnames.org/) ends the debate in seconds — and it's actually fun to watch.

[Wheel of Names](https://wheelnames.org/) is a free online [wheel spinner](https://wheelnames.org/) that does exactly this. Type or paste your list — names, teams, prizes, tasks — and spin. The wheel animates with sound and lands on one random pick.

## Why teachers love it

Calling on students fairly, forming random groups, picking helpers — the [name picker wheel](https://wheelnames.org/) makes it transparent. Students see the spin happen live, so there's no argument about favoritism.

## Great for giveaways

Running a raffle or a stream giveaway? Put every entrant on the wheel and spin it live. The animated reveal builds suspense your audience will enjoy.

## How it works

1. Open the page
2. Add your entries (paste a list to go fast)
3. Hit spin — done. Every spin is random and fair.

If you constantly make small group decisions, keep a [decision wheel](https://wheelnames.org/) bookmarked. [Spin the wheel online](https://wheelnames.org/) whenever you need a fast, fair pick.
